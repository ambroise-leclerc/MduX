# ISO 14971:2019 §10 — Production and post-production activities

## §10.1 General

Establishing a system to collect and review production and post-production information, feeding
back into the risk management process. Device-level, and dependent on there being a released device
generating field information — MduX has neither yet.

## §10.2 Collection of information

Sources named by the standard include production process monitoring, user feedback, complaints, and
publicly available information about similar devices. MduX's nearest analog is far narrower: its own
CI results and issue tracker are production information about *this repository*, not about a
released device. See [`../iec62304/07-problem-resolution-process.md`](../iec62304/07-problem-resolution-process.md)
and [`../iso13485/06-measurement-analysis-improvement.md`](../iso13485/06-measurement-analysis-improvement.md)
for where this corpus already discusses that mechanism and its limits — repeating it here would be
exactly the kind of citation stretched to fit this convention rejects.

## §10.3 Review of information

Evaluating whether new information affects previously accepted risk — whether a hazard was
missed, a risk estimate was wrong, or a new hazard has emerged. Device-level; no MduX mechanism.

## §10.4 Actions

Acting on that review: updating the risk management file, or taking field action if a device
already in use presents unacceptable risk. Device-level; no MduX mechanism, and no field to act on.
